## Eiger design

The combat-state module owns health, shields/armor, damage events, mitigation, healing, downed state hooks, death, respawn requests, damage feedback, and kill attribution. Damage delivery comes from weapons, abilities, hazards, AI, or world events through one validated contract.

## Configuration

Health profiles define maximum values, regeneration, armor behavior, friendly-fire policy, death policy, respawn delay, and whether an experience uses lethal damage at all.

## Phase

Health, damage, death, respawn, and feedback are P0. Downed/revive, limb damage, status effects, and armor plates are P1 modules.
