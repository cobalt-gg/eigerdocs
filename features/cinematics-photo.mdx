## Scope

Sequencer handoff, cinematic cameras, skip policy, subtitles, letterboxing, screenshot capture, free camera, poses, filters, and privacy-safe photo sharing.

## Design

Cinematics request input/UI/camera states through defined contracts and always restore gameplay safely. Photo mode remains a local presentation tool unless an experience explicitly supports it.
