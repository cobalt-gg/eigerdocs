title: "Communication and squad tools"
description: "Optional social coordination tools with accessibility and safety built in."
---

## Scope

Text chat, voice chat, proximity chat, pings, radial callouts, squad markers, map drawing, quick chat, captions, mute, block, report, and privacy controls.

## Design

Communication providers are adapters so a project can choose an approved service. The shared interface delivers events to UI, objectives, and accessibility layers.

## Phase

P1 for pings and callouts; hosted voice/text and moderation are P3 operational systems.
