## Eiger design

Item Definitions describe what an item is. Pickup actors describe how it appears and is claimed in the world. Equipment slots describe how a carried item affects the player. Loot sources use tables and rules to produce eligible items without hard-coding a genre.

## Examples

Ammo and weapon pickups suit an arena. Loadout crates suit tactical rounds. Chests and randomized containers suit larger maps. Keys and quest items suit stories. Each is a configured producer of the same item contract.

## Phase

Basic pickup and equipment flow is P0. Randomized loot tables, rarity, containers, and persistent world loot are P1/P2.
