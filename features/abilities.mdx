## Player promise

If a game enables abilities, you understand availability, cost, cooldown, targeting, activation feedback, and counterplay. An ultimate is simply an ability with a different acquisition and presentation policy.

## Eiger design

Use Unreal's Gameplay Ability System for attributes, gameplay effects, tags, prediction, cooldowns, costs, and replication. `EigerAbilities` supplies common activation and UI contracts; individual Cobalt games define their abilities and balance.

## Configuration

Ability definitions declare tags, input, targeter, costs, cooldowns, charges, effects, cancellation, unlock source, and whether they occupy an ability or ultimate slot.

## Phase

Build the module in P1 after weapons and networking are stable. Do not require abilities for experiences that only need conventional shooting or narrative interaction.
