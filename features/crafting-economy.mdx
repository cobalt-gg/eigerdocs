## Scope

Resources, recipes, workbenches, repair, recycling, vendors, currency, barter, insurance, stash, mail, and secure inventory.

## Design

Recipes and transactions use item definitions and versioned profile payloads. The economy never belongs in the baseline shooter loop.

## Phase

P2/P3, after inventory and persistent profile rules are stable.
