## Scope

Footsteps by material, weapon and impact variants, near-miss feedback, occlusion, reverb, music states, announcers, dialogue, radio, dynamic mix, and visual sound cues.

## Design

Gameplay emits semantic audio events; experiences supply sound banks and mix policies. UI and accessibility can translate important sound events into captions or indicators.

## Phase

P1 for key combat and interaction feedback. Adaptive music and complex propagation are P2.
