## Purpose

Gadgets cover throwable, deployable, scanning, traversal, support, and utility tools: grenades, smoke, traps, shields, sensors, breaching tools, and grapples.

## Eiger design

Gadgets are equipment items with targeting, charges, cooldown or consumable rules, deployment validation, and clear world representation. They use the ability/effect layer only when a gadget benefits from shared targeting, costs, or status effects.

## Phase

One throwable and one deployable reference gadget arrive after P0 weapons. Full gadget inventories, crafting, and class-specific kits are P1 experience work.
