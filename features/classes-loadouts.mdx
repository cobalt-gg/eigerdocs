## Scope

Classes, heroes, operators, factions, role kits, passive traits, perk choices, preset loadouts, drafts, bans, and round buy rules.

## Design

A Role Definition grants allowed equipment, abilities, movement modifiers, cosmetics, and UI presentation. It composes existing modules rather than becoming a replacement player type.
