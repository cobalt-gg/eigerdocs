## Player promise

An experience can be first-person, third-person, or let you switch views without rebuilding the pawn or weapon system.

## Eiger design

`EigerCamera` provides Camera Profile Data Assets. A profile controls view mode, boom distance, offsets, field of view, shoulder selection, collision behavior, look limits, shake policy, and optional sprint/aim changes.

The character exposes camera anchors and gameplay signals. Camera logic never decides damage, animation, or weapon rules.

## Configuration

Ship first-person and third-person profiles first. Add aim, spectator, vehicle, and cinematic profiles later as distinct assets.

## Rules and phase

Camera state is normally local. Only replicate a view choice when other players need it for animation or gameplay. Core profiles are P0.
