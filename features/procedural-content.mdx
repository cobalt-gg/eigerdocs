## Scope

Seeded layouts, procedural encounters, loot placement, contracts, dungeon rooms, daily runs, biome rules, and validation of generated content.

## Design

Generators must expose deterministic seeds, constraints, debug views, replay information, and authored fallback content. They are consumers of map, item, and encounter contracts.

## Phase

P3 unless procedural generation is the first game’s core fantasy.
