---
title: "Ballistics and weapon simulation"
description: "Optional fidelity systems for projects that need more than basic hitscan and projectiles."
---

## Scope

Projectile drop, travel time, wind, drag, penetration, ricochet, material response, armor interaction, suppression, heat, jams, durability, and firing modes.

## Design

These are selectable firing and impact policies on a weapon definition. Keep the simple arcade path fast and predictable; enable simulation only per experience or weapon family.

## Phase

P2 after profiling, because high-fidelity simulation affects networking, AI, VFX, audio, and performance.
