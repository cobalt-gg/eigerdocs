---
title: "Vehicles"
description: "Optional drivable and rideable vehicles for traversal, combat, and world simulation."
---

## Scope

Cars, bikes, aircraft, boats, mounts, turrets, passenger seats, vehicle weapons, fuel, damage, repair, cargo, and vehicle camera profiles.

## Design

Vehicles implement an enter/exit, seat, ownership, damage, and input contract. A game can add a vehicle type without making vehicle support a character dependency.

## Phase

P3 unless a first Cobalt game depends on it. Vehicle prediction and replication are major technical work.
