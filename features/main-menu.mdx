## Purpose

The front end gets you into the appropriate experience without coupling Eiger to a single game or online provider.

## Eiger design

The base shell supports boot, profile selection, play, settings, credits, legal text, loading, error recovery, and return-to-menu behavior. Cobalt games provide branding, background scenes, play destinations, news, social, and store pages as optional extensions.

## Phase

A minimal menu, pause flow, loading screen, and settings route are P0. Accounts, social, news, storefronts, and season screens are P3 service integrations.
