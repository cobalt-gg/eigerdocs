## Scope

Platform achievements, badges, daily/weekly challenges, mastery tracks, match history, personal bests, leaderboards, heat maps, and shareable stats.

## Design

Systems consume verified gameplay events. Progress tracking is versioned and can be local, platform-backed, or service-backed.

## Phase

P2 for local challenges and history; P3 for global leaderboards and live-service loops.
