## Scope

Observer cameras, follow/free cameras, delay, killcam, replay capture, highlight markers, director controls, tournament lobbies, and broadcast overlays.

## Design

Spectator state follows match policy and privacy rules. Replay and tournament layers consume gameplay events rather than altering core combat.

## Phase

P2 for observer mode and replay experiments; advanced broadcasts are P3.
