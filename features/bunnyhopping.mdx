title: "Bunnyhopping"

---

## Player promise

If enabled, well-timed jumps and air steering preserve or build momentum within the game’s chosen limits. If disabled, jumping remains conventional and predictable.

## Eiger design

`EigerBunnyhop` adds a movement modifier rather than replacing movement. It reads landing timing, horizontal speed, air control, and optional strafe input. Other systems can react to its movement state without knowing its math.

## Configuration

A Bunnyhop Profile defines grace window, speed cap, gain/loss curves, friction rules, repeated-jump behavior, landing assist, and whether the system is visible in tutorials.

## Multiplayer and phase

The server remains authoritative over speed. The module needs prediction tests at high latency and anti-macro-friendly limits. It is P2 because its tuning strongly shapes a game’s identity.
