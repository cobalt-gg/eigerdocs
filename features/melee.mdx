---

## Scope

Quick melee, charged strikes, combos, blocking, parries, executions, takedowns, thrown weapons, and nonlethal options.

## Design

Melee is a weapon-delivery variant using timing windows, hit traces, animation events, target rules, and the shared damage contract. It must not force a combo system onto firearms-only games.

## Phase

Basic quick melee is P1; parry/combos are P2 and require latency-aware validation.
