---
title: "Difficulty and player assists"
description: "Configurable support that respects both the intended experience and individual player needs."
---
## Scope

Difficulty presets, adaptive difficulty, checkpoint frequency, navigation assist, puzzle hints, aim assistance, invulnerability options, slower game speed, and content warnings.

## Design

An experience declares what assists are compatible with its rules and whether they affect matchmaking, rewards, or achievements. Accessibility support should never be treated as a cheat by default.

## Phase

P1 for single-player and co-op; competitive policies are per-experience.
