## Eiger design

Each map declares an Experience Data Asset, a map policy, spawn sets, level-flow settings, and optional streaming layers. Eiger validates that selected modules and map requirements agree before play begins.

## Supported patterns

- Small arena maps with direct match startup.
- Round-based maps with staging and observer spaces.
- Story maps with checkpoints and travel flow.
- Large streamed worlds only after a concrete scale target exists.

## Phase

Foundation and arena test maps are P0. World Partition policy, procedural maps, and battle royale scale are P2/P3 decisions driven by a real game.
