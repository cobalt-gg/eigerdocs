## Scope

Custom mode definitions, maps, server rules, content packs, publishing, dependencies, permissions, version compatibility, and moderation.

## Design

Creator-facing contracts must be intentional from the beginning: stable public APIs, data validation, isolated content, and clear multiplayer authority. Do not expose internal framework classes as the modding API.

## Phase

P3. It becomes a product strategy, security commitment, and support commitment—not just a feature toggle.
