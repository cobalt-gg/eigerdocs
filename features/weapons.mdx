## Player promise

An experience can define what you carry—primary weapon, secondary weapon, melee option, or no weapon—and every item has readable, responsive behavior.

## Eiger design

`EigerWeapons` separates a Weapon Definition from its firing, ammo, reload, recoil, spread, equip, animation, and cosmetic behavior. Primary and secondary are equipment-slot policies, not hard-coded weapon classes. Support hitscan, projectile, beam, shotgun, and melee delivery through one damage contract.

## Configuration

Weapon Data Assets define slot eligibility, fire modes, ammo source, reload policy, spread/recoil curves, range, damage delivery, attachments, sounds, effects, and AI-facing traits.

## Rules and phase

Primary/secondary selection, hitscan, projectile, reload, ammo, and swap flow are P0. Advanced ballistics, penetration, charge mechanics, and weapon crafting are later modules. Damage is server-authoritative.
