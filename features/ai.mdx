## Eiger design

AI integration provides team affiliation, perception events, a combat/interaction interface, navigation hooks, spawn points, and debug visibility. Behavior trees, EQS, and StateTree implementations remain interchangeable behind those contracts.

## Encounters

An encounter asset defines participating archetypes, spawn rules, activation, completion conditions, difficulty knobs, and rewards. It must work for combat, stealth, narrative, and co-op scenarios.

## Phase

Interfaces and spawn points are P1. Full bot suites, squads, tactical planning, and an encounter director are P2.
