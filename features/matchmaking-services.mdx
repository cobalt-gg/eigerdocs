description: "Provider-agnostic online infrastructure for multiplayer products."
---
## Scope

Identity, sessions, lobbies, matchmaking, parties, invites, dedicated servers, reconnect, cross-play, entitlements, anti-cheat, reporting, and moderation.

## Design

Gameplay communicates through service interfaces. No weapon, objective, or character module may depend on one provider’s SDK.

## Phase

P0 proves basic session hosting; full services are P3 and need explicit operational ownership.
