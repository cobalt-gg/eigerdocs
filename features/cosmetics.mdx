title: "Customization and cosmetics"
description: "Optional player expression with clear separation from gameplay balance."
---

## Scope

Character appearance, weapon skins, charms, gear, emotes, banners, player cards, voice packs, animation sets, and UI themes.

## Design

Cosmetics attach presentation assets to cosmetic slots. Gameplay-affecting equipment is always a separate definition and cannot be hidden in a cosmetic payload.

## Phase

P2 for local customization; storefronts, entitlements, and seasonal delivery are P3.
