## Scope

Breakable props, doors, windows, lights, cover, destructible buildings, physical debris, explosions, and interactable physics objects.

## Design

Destruction is authored as map content with gameplay tags and state events. Experiences decide what is cosmetic, server-relevant, persistent, or reset between rounds.

## Phase

P2/P3. Prototype small, replicated breakables first; do not promise full environmental destruction before scale and server budgets are known.
