title: "Advanced traversal"
description: "Optional movement extensions for games that need more than standard locomotion."
---

## Scope

Slides, mantles, vaults, ledge grabs, climbing, ladders, swimming, diving, dashes, dodges, double jumps, grapples, zip lines, launch pads, and zero gravity are independently enabled movement capabilities.

## Design

Each extension is a character add-on with its own profile, animation signals, camera hooks, surface rules, stamina/cooldown policy, and multiplayer prediction requirements. No experience receives advanced traversal by accident.

## Phase

Prototype one add-on at a time after base movement. Ship only options a reference experience proves fun and network-safe.
