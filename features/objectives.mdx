## Eiger design

Teams use a shared affiliation contract. Objective actors publish state, eligibility, progress, and completion events. Scoring and victory rules consume those events through an experience-defined match policy.

## Supported patterns

Kill score, score limit, capture, plant/defuse, extraction, survival, escort, collection, co-op completion, and noncompetitive story objectives can share the same event vocabulary while keeping their rules separate.

## Phase

Teams, score, timer, and a simple objective are P0. Round economy, ranked rules, spectators, and complex multi-stage objectives are P1.
