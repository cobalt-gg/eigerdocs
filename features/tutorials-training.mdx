## Scope

Contextual hints, guided objectives, practice range, bot drills, movement trials, firing tests, codex entries, coaching prompts, and first-session flow.

## Design

Tutorial steps listen to gameplay events and objective state. They are content-driven, skippable, repeatable, and accessible; they do not hard-code a single game’s campaign.

## Phase

Basic prompts are P1; authored tutorials and mastery training are P2.
