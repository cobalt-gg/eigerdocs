## Scope

Visibility, light exposure, noise propagation, suspicion, alert escalation, disguises, takedowns, hiding spots, distractions, alarms, and nonlethal outcomes.

## Design

Eiger publishes visibility and noise events. AI, missions, UI, and abilities consume those events without a fixed stealth game rule set. An experience selects detection thresholds and failure policy.

## Phase

P2. Build against a narrative or infiltration sample, not as an assumed property of every character.
