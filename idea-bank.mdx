## Idea graduation checklist

Move an idea into a feature page when you can answer:

1. Which Cobalt experience needs it first?
2. Is it core, optional, or game-specific?
3. What player problem or fantasy does it serve?
4. What does it depend on, and what must not depend on it?
5. What is the smallest playable proof?
6. How does it behave in multiplayer, saves, UI, accessibility, and performance tests?
