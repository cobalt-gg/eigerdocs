## Behavior
An experience declares slots such as primary, secondary, melee, gadget, ultimate, armor, backpack, key item, and quick slot; items declare valid slots.
## Eiger contract
Uses inventory and equipment. Configure slot count, exclusivity, swap time, hotkeys, visibility, and death behavior. It does not require every listed slot.
