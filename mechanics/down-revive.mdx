## Behavior
At zero health, enter downed state; bleed out, crawl, receive assistance, revive, or finish according to the experience policy.
## Eiger contract
Uses damage, interaction, teams, animation, UI, and match rules. Configure bleed-out, revive duration, items, limits, execution rules, and respawn relation. Requires multiplayer authority.
