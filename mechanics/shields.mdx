## Player promise

You know whether you are protected, how much protection remains, whether it is recharging, what broke it, and how to restore it. Shield feedback is immediate and readable.

## Shield models

- Regenerating energy shield with a damage-delay recharge.
- Finite armor/shield points restored by pickups or consumables.
- Directional shield with front, side, and rear values.
- Temporary ability barrier, personal bubble, or deployable cover.
- Overshield that decays or expires.
- Damage-type resistance shield, such as energy, fire, gas, or radiation protection.

## Eiger contract

`EigerShields` is a combat-state layer resolved before health. A Shield Profile defines capacity, layers, damage absorption, recharge delay/rate, break threshold, restoration methods, damage-type rules, and feedback. It can work without inventory through automatic regeneration, or with inventory through batteries/plates.

Shields are separate from the broader [armor mechanic](/mechanics/armor): armor can be static mitigation or consumable plates, while shields commonly absorb damage and recharge. An experience can use either, both, or neither.

## Multiplayer rules

The server resolves shield absorption, depletion, recharge eligibility, and restoration. Clients can predict cosmetic feedback but do not decide whether a shield saved damage. Compact shield state replicates only to viewers who need it.

## Dependencies and phase

Requires damage, health, UI, and multiplayer. A basic regenerating shield is P0/P1 depending on the reference experience. Directional, deployable, and damage-type variants are P1/P2.

## Acceptance checks

- Damage consistently resolves through shields before health when configured.
- Recharge pauses/restarts according to the profile and remains synchronized online.
- Break, restore, and low-shield states are accessible without relying only on color or sound.
- Healing, armor, status effects, and abilities cannot bypass configured shield policy accidentally.
