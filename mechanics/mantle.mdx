title: "Mantle"
description: "A short traversal action that moves a character over a valid ledge."
---
## Behavior
Detect a reachable surface, test clearance, select a low/high mantle, then transition through a controlled movement and animation sequence.
## Eiger contract
Uses surface queries, character collision, movement state, animation, and server validation. Configure reach, height, clearance, duration, stamina, and allowed materials. Requires base movement.
