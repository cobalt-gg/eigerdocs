## Behavior
Trigger a conversation, present lines and choices, gate options by state, execute consequences, and persist relevant outcomes.
## Eiger contract
Uses interaction, UI, subtitles, quest events, and saves. Configure speaker, lines, choices, conditions, skip policy, and multiplayer ownership. Requires narrative content.
