title: "Drop and redeploy"
description: "Controlled insertion and return mechanics for large-scale or squad modes."
---
## Behavior
Select or receive an insertion route, deploy safely, and optionally return through tickets, teammates, stations, or timed waves.
## Eiger contract
Uses maps, spawning, squads, vehicles/cinematics, and match rules. Configure routes, landing rules, redeploy resource, safety, and late-join policy. Requires large-scale experience design.
