## Behavior
When activated, capture approved state; on failure or reload, restore the player, world, objectives, inventory, and rules permitted by the experience.
## Eiger contract
Uses saves, maps, objectives, and inventory. Configure capture fields, autosave timing, retry, difficulty relation, and migration. Requires versioned profile data.
