## Behavior
Target world space or an actor, choose a contextual callout, transmit to eligible players, and expire or update the marker.
## Eiger contract
Uses input, UI, teams, map markers, and communication. Configure categories, duration, visibility, cooldown, and accessibility captions. Suitable for P1 multiplayer.
