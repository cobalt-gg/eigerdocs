## Behavior
A companion receives follow, hold, assist, revive, interact, and story directives while observing pathing and combat safety rules.
## Eiger contract
Uses AI, teams, interaction, dialogue, damage, and saves. Configure role, command set, mortality, relationship, and equipment. Requires AI and narrative/co-op policy.
