## Player promise

You can see what is being ranked, the season or time window, your rank and percentile, how rankings are calculated, and when results update.

## Leaderboard types

- Personal bests: fastest route, highest score, longest streak.
- Global or regional rankings: score, wins, rating, time, or challenge completion.
- Friends, clan, squad, seasonal, event, and platform-specific views.
- Competitive rating ladders with placement, promotion, decay, and anti-abuse policy.
- Creator or custom-server listings, if modding is supported.

## Eiger contract

`EigerLeaderboards` is a provider adapter, not a database. It accepts a server-verified Result Record after a match or challenge, then lets a selected platform or service store/query ranked data. The UI uses a common result/query model so providers can be swapped.

Define ranking metric, eligibility, region, season, reset date, tie-breaker, privacy rules, anti-cheat requirement, and update cadence in a Leaderboard Definition.

## Integrity and privacy

Never submit a client-claimed score. Competitive leaderboards require server verification, abuse monitoring, result replay/audit data where feasible, and a removal/appeal policy. Respect opt-out, display-name, age, regional, and account-linking requirements.

## Dependencies and phase

Requires scoring or challenge results, profile/identity, UI, and an online service. Personal bests can be P1 with local or platform storage. Global, seasonal, and ranked ladders are P3 live-service work.

## Acceptance checks

- Results include a verifiable source match/challenge record.
- Duplicate submissions do not create duplicate rankings.
- Season reset and tie-break behavior are documented and testable.
- Unavailable service states degrade gracefully without blocking normal play.
