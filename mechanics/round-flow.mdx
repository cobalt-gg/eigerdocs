## Behavior
Transition through warmup, ready check, buy/loadout, active round, resolution, observer, intermission, and match-end phases.
## Eiger contract
Uses experience, teams, objectives, UI, spawning, and sessions. Configure phase order, timers, allowed actions, economy reset, overtime, and rematch. Requires a match policy.
