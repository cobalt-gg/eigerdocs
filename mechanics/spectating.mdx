## Behavior
Move between permitted targets/cameras, observe allowed information, and transition safely when spawning, reviving, or leaving.
## Eiger contract
Uses camera, match flow, teams, UI, and privacy policy. Configure delay, target rules, free camera, enemy information, and broadcast mode. Requires multiplayer game rules.
