## Behavior
Enter a slide from sprinting or a slope; preserve momentum according to the active Movement Profile; exit through duration, speed, collision, jump, or player input.
## Eiger contract
Uses character state, movement prediction, camera signals, and animation events. Configure entry speed, duration, friction, slope response, hitbox, and cancel policy. Requires base movement; it is optional.
