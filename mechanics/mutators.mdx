title: "Mutators"
description: "Composable rule overrides for custom games, events, testing, and playful variants."
---
## Behavior
Enable a named rule modifier at experience startup and publish its changes to gameplay, UI, and validation without editing base mode code.
## Eiger contract
Mutators may alter movement, weapons, damage, inventory, objectives, and cosmetics only through public policies. Configure compatibility, priority, conflicts, and multiplayer restrictions. Requires experiences.
