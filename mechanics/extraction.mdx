## Behavior
Reach an eligible exit, satisfy conditions, begin an extract timer, defend or cancel, then persist approved rewards on success.
## Eiger contract
Uses objectives, inventory, multiplayer, and save/profile rules. Configure exit availability, timer, noise, capacity, failure, and reward persistence. Requires server authority.
