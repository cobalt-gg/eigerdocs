## Behavior
Lean offsets the camera and posture around cover; prone changes capsule, speed, aiming, and clearance rules.
## Eiger contract
Uses stance, camera, collision, animation, and weapon restrictions. Configure angles, transition time, clearance, accuracy modifiers, and allowed terrain. Requires character and camera modules.
