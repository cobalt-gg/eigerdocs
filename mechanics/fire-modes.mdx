## Behavior
Each trigger action resolves through the active mode, respecting cadence, charge, burst count, heat, ammo, and interruption rules.
## Eiger contract
Fire Mode assets plug into a Weapon Definition. Configure activation type, cadence, ammo cost, release policy, and feedback. Requires weapons and server-authoritative firing.
