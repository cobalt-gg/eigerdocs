## Behavior
Publish the active safe area, warn players, damage or penalize out-of-bounds players, and change phases according to match timing.
## Eiger contract
Uses map bounds, damage, UI, objectives, and multiplayer replication. Configure shapes, path, timings, damage, preview, and final-circle policy. Requires large-scale mode rules.
