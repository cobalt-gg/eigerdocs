## Behavior
Enter a valid seat, grant its input/camera/weapon permissions, synchronize occupancy, and exit safely at an approved location.
## Eiger contract
Uses interaction, vehicles, input, camera, damage, and multiplayer. Configure seat roles, capacity, exit points, weapon access, and seat switching. Requires a vehicle actor.
