---
title: "Reload"
description: "A configurable ammo transfer action with tactical and arcade variants."
---
## Behavior
Start reload, lock or allow cancellation per weapon, transfer magazine/reserve ammo at the chosen event, and publish completion or interruption feedback.
## Eiger contract
Uses weapon state, inventory ammo, animation events, and UI. Configure tactical versus empty reload, per-shell behavior, speed, cancel points, and retained magazine policy. Requires weapons and inventory.
