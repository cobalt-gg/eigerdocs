## Behavior
Firing applies camera/weapon recoil and a deterministic or random spread policy; movement, stance, heat, and aiming can modify both.
## Eiger contract
Weapon assets supply curves and modifiers. Configure recovery, patterns, bloom, ADS multipliers, and assist interactions. Requires weapons, camera, and an agreed competitive policy.
