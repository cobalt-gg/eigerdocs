---
title: "Map voting"
description: "A player choice mechanic for selecting the next allowed map or experience."
---
## Behavior
Present eligible options, collect votes within a phase, resolve ties deterministically, then travel while respecting rotation and party rules.
## Eiger contract
Uses front end, match flow, sessions, maps, and UI. Configure candidate selection, vote time, weighting, cooldowns, and host/server policy. Requires multiple playable maps.
