## Behavior
Enter water, transition to swim physics, optionally dive and manage breath, then exit at a valid shoreline or ladder.
## Eiger contract
Uses volume detection, movement mode, camera, audio, and damage/status hooks. Configure speed, buoyancy, breath, currents, visibility, and equipment restrictions. Requires water-enabled maps.
