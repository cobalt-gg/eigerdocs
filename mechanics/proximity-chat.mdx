## Behavior
Route voice by distance, team, location, state, and privacy settings; display speaking indicators and allow immediate mute/report actions.
## Eiger contract
Uses a provider adapter, sessions, UI, accessibility, and moderation. Configure ranges, channels, dead-player policy, push-to-talk, captions, and parental restrictions. Requires operations ownership.
