## Player promise

You can understand the current score, how it changed, what earns points, and how close you are to the win condition. The scoreboard is accurate for every player.

## What scoring supports

- eliminations, assists, deaths, streaks, and objective actions;
- capture, escort, delivery, survival, extraction, and round wins;
- individual, team, squad, faction, and co-op contribution totals;
- positive/negative scoring, multipliers, bonuses, penalties, and tie-breakers;
- visible scoreboards, compact HUD score, post-match summaries, and event feed entries.

## Eiger contract

`EigerScoring` consumes verified gameplay events and turns them into Score Events. An Experience-owned Scoring Policy defines event values, recipients, display rules, score cap, tie-break rules, and relation to match victory. It does not assume that kills are valuable or even possible.

For example, an arena mode awards eliminations. A rescue game awards safe evacuations. A narrative game may use no visible score at all.

## Multiplayer rules

Only the server accepts score events and updates player/team score state. Clients receive replicated score summaries and cosmetic event messages. Awarding must be idempotent so replays, reconnects, or replicated events cannot duplicate points.

## Dependencies and phase

Requires experiences, objectives, teams/squads where relevant, UI, and multiplayer. Basic kill/objective/team score is P0. Detailed contribution, assist attribution, medals, and post-match breakdowns are P1.

## Acceptance checks

- Score changes once and only once for a verified event.
- Individual, team, and squad totals agree with the scoreboard.
- Score caps, overtime, ties, and negative scores resolve deterministically.
- The UI offers a non-color-only indication of rank and team state.
