## Player promise

You can always understand where safety is now, where it will be next, how long remains in the current phase, how dangerous the storm is, and whether you have a realistic route to survive.

## What makes it more than a safe zone

The [safe zone](/mechanics/safe-zone) defines the legal playable region. The storm system directs the entire battle royale pacing around that region:

- chooses the initial and subsequent zone centers, radii, and phase timings;
- transitions between a waiting period and a shrinking period;
- applies escalating damage or other out-of-zone consequences;
- controls map, HUD, audio, VFX, lighting, and announcer cues;
- protects against impossible endings, unreachable zones, and unfair final circles;
- publishes phase information for loot, contracts, AI, vehicles, and spectator systems.

## Phase model

Each Storm Phase Data Asset includes:

| Setting | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Safe radius | Final size of the playable area for this phase. |
| Wait time | Time players have to reposition before contraction begins. |
| Shrink time | Time used to move from the current radius to the next radius. |
| Damage model | Damage per second, tick rate, ramp, or non-damage penalty. |
| Center selection | Random, weighted, authored, or constrained destination policy. |
| Endgame policy | Minimum radius, moving zone, sudden death, or objective finish. |
| Presentation profile | Map color, audio intensity, HUD warnings, and accessibility cues. |

An experience combines phase assets into a Storm Schedule. Small custom modes can use two phases. A full battle royale can use a long schedule with early exploration, midgame rotation, and tight endgame pressure.

## Fairness rules

- Generate only reachable next zones; reject water, blocked terrain, or otherwise invalid destinations.
- Provide enough warning time for the configured map size and expected mobility.
- Use server-authoritative zone math and replicate a compact phase state to clients.
- Avoid revealing future circles before the experience’s intended scouting or information rules permit it.
- Test all phases with walking, vehicle, traversal, downed, and late-join scenarios.
- Give players non-audio indicators for every critical storm warning.

## Eiger architecture

`EigerBattleRoyaleStorm` is an optional module above `EigerExperience`, map flow, objectives, damage, UI, audio, and multiplayer. It must not become a dependency of tactical, arena, or narrative modes.

Public events include phase announced, contraction started, player entered storm, player exited storm, final phase started, and storm resolved. Other modules subscribe to these events instead of inspecting storm internals.

## Configuration extensions

- **Storm damage:** health damage, shield drain, healing reduction, visibility loss, movement penalty, or inventory decay.
- **Moving endgame:** a target path or moving circle that prevents static final-cover stalemates.
- **Multiple hazards:** fire, radiation, flood, cold, gas, darkness, or a story-specific anomaly.
- **Reveals:** late-phase scan pulses, marked leaders, dynamic objectives, or final extraction.
- **Accessibility:** high-contrast edge, safe-direction indicator, timing text, captions, and adjustable visual intensity.

## Dependencies and delivery

Requires a large-scale map policy, spawning/drop flow, server-authoritative damage, map/HUD presentation, and multiplayer performance budgets. Prototype with a simple circular gas zone only after the two-player combat loop is stable. Add constrained placements, moving zones, terrain validation, and full presentation only when a battle royale Cobalt experience is funded.

## Acceptance checks

- Every client agrees on current/next phase and storm position.
- Storm damage is never client-authoritative.
- The schedule cannot choose an invalid or unreachable final zone.
- A player has clear visual, audio, and non-audio feedback before and during danger.
- Spectators and replays reproduce storm state correctly.
- A load test meets replication and frame-time budgets at the intended player count.
