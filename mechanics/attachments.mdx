---
title: "Weapon attachments"
description: "Composable weapon modifiers such as sights, magazines, barrels, and underbarrel tools."
---
## Behavior
Attach a compatible item to a socket, apply allowed stat/behavior changes, and update visuals and UI.
## Eiger contract
Uses inventory, weapons, and Data Assets. Configure compatibility tags, modifier stacking, visual sockets, unlock rules, and mid-match edit policy. Requires weapon definitions.
