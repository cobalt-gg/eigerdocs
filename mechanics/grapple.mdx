## Behavior
Acquire a valid anchor, fire a tether, pull the player or target, and release on input, collision, range, or timeout.
## Eiger contract
Can be an equipment item or ability. Configure anchor tags, range, pull curve, reel behavior, cooldown, and PvP policy. Requires targeting, movement prediction, and multiplayer anti-exploit checks.
