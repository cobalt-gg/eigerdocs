## Behavior
Interact with an available line, attach at a valid point, travel according to line direction/rules, and detach through end, jump, or input.
## Eiger contract
Uses interaction, map actors, movement state, and camera signals. Configure speed, two-way policy, capacity, combat permissions, and detach impulse. Requires map flow and base movement.
