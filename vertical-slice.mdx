## Reference experience: arena skirmish

The first slice is a compact two-player arena shooter: one map, two weapon archetypes, two spawn areas, and a score limit. Its job is to validate reusable systems, not to become a full game.

## Player journey

1. Launch into a main menu and adjust input, audio, display, subtitles, and accessibility settings.
2. Start or join a two-player local/network session.
3. Select a first-person or third-person camera profile.
4. Spawn with a primary weapon and find a pickup.
5. Fight, take damage, die, respawn, and see score feedback.
6. Finish the round and return to the menu with settings preserved.

## Required systems

- Enhanced Input and prompts for keyboard/mouse and controller.
- Configurable character movement, camera, health, damage, and death.
- Weapon and item Data Assets.
- Hitscan rifle and projectile launcher with replicated damage.
- Pickups, inventory slots, HUD, score, timer, and match state.
- Listen-server flow and two-client test coverage.
- Debug overlays for network role, tags, health, weapon state, and latency.

## Explicitly exclude

- accounts, ranked play, matchmaking, commerce, battle pass, or anti-cheat;
- full bot behaviors, large maps, battle royale zones, vehicles, crafting, or destruction;
- final art, final animation sets, and platform certification work.

## Acceptance checklist

- The same weapon and player systems operate in both camera views.
- A designer can create a weapon variation without changing C++.
- The match completes over a networked two-player session.
- Damage and score are server-authoritative.
- Rebinding, subtitles, text scale, and reduced-motion settings persist after restart.
- The sample map explains how each Eiger module is used.
